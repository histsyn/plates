## Notes

- **Production files** (the ones wired into the paper and the post):
  `cm_1869_04_24_col.jpg`, `gaz1870_votes_hl.jpg`,
  `gaz1870_passage_hl_hl.jpg`, `gaz1872_auditors_hl.jpg`,
  `sp1896_opening_hl.jpg`, `gaz1896_commission_hl.jpg`.
  Broadpage exception: the *China Mail* plate is a **column crop**
  (`_col`), per §6B — the full newspaper sheet is unreadable at plate size.
- **No image twin exists** for the promulgated Ordinance No. 3 of 1870
  itself (Gazette 2 April 1870, printed pp. 151–153): four phrase sweeps of
  the image series ("VICTORIE REGINE", "for establishing a Chinese
  Hospital to be supported", "DONATION of Fifteen thousand Dollars",
  "Eleemosynary Corporation" restricted to 1870 titles) returned no scan of
  those leaves — the ordinance text is cited text-only (`/page/` ids
  1676710–1676712). The passage-day V&P plate (`gaz1870_passage_hl_hl.jpg`)
  carries the ordinance's title and number in the period's own print.
- **Printed vs index numbers**: every citation in the notes/post/paper
  uses the printed page read off the scan (56, 156, 182, [XLVII], 1149);
  index labels that disagree (image title "Page 57", "1896 - Page 1443")
  are registered as variance, not cited.
- Highlight boxes were seated by `tools/scan_highlights.py` (drift
  re-seat: 30 Mar plate −21 px onto its ink band) and **confirmed by eye**
  at the final crop; the *China Mail* column crop was cut and boxed by
  hand (`/tmp/opencode/fb/tw1872/col_plate.py`).
- No web photographs are used in this thread; all six plates are corpus
  scans. `proposed_potential_image_source.txt` not needed — the image pass
  was completed during the research run itself (§2 mandate).
