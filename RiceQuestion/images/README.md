# RiceQuestion — image manifest (§6B gate record)

All plates are **column crops of newspaper pages** (the §6B newspaper rule)
taken from the China Mail's 1919 image-series leaves between 26 July and
30 July 1919. Every production file below was read back by the session
model's own eyes at its final bytes (gate passed 2026-11-03): right
document/issue/date, highlight boxes on the intended lines, crops complete,
captions = what the scans print.

**Working files kept for reference (not published):** the untouched scans
(`cm_1919-07-26_p8.jpg` etc.), the tool outputs (`*_hl.jpg` full pages with
boxes, one v1/v2 pair), and the probe crops used to seat the boxes. The
untouched scans are kept so any plate can be re-cropped.

**Notes.**
* The 26 July leaf is the first report ("later edition" of that Saturday);
  the 28 July leaf carries the full weekend report, the Shaukiwan shooting
  and the court column; the 30 July leaf carries the press battle letters.
* Crop edges: the plates keep the full column width; thin slivers of the
  neighbouring column remain at the right edge of some crops (standard for
  a column crop) and the neighbouring text is unrelated to the quotes.
* The *Daily Press* and *Telegraph* have no 1919 image series — their
  quotations in the paper are text-only (public `/page/` links), marked in
  the notes where the OCR had to be used.
* The Chinese-language relief photograph noticed in the Telegraph's
  pictorial supplement of 16 August 1919 ("One of the Free Co[ngee
  stations]", photo by Mee Chiong) has **no image twin** and is therefore
  not plated (recorded as gap G6).
