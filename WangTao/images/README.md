## The row offset on the 1881 plate (recorded, not a defect)

The English list and the Chinese list on the 1881 leaf are set independently:
the Chinese block carries its own 「簽名」 head row and therefore sits about one
row lower on the page. The correspondence is by **ordinal**, not by baseline —
Wong T'ò and 王韜 are the 13th entry in their respective columns (Wai Yuk/韋玉
= 1st in both), and the leaf's own translator (E. J. Eitel) certifies the list.
The plate's single box therefore encloses the neighbouring line as well; the
caption states the ordinal pairing.

## Registered image-index variance

The gazette image series titles are internal offsets, not printed folios. Read
off the leaves: 1491235 prints p. 189 (title "1880 - Page 189"); 1491357 prints
**311** (title "1880 - Page 311"); 1504953 prints **276** (title
"1881 - Page 280" — offset by 4). The captions cite the printed folios.
