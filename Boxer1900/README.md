# Boxer1900 — plates

Document plates for the paper *The Colony in the Boxer Summer: Hong Kong in
1900*. Every plate was read back by eye at its final bytes before it was
accepted; the highlighted boxes are the ones the paper quotes.

Access date for every corpus link: **7 November 2026**.

| File | Source | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `plate_cm0611_taku_col.jpg` | *China Mail*, 11 June 1900, p. 3 — https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/image-page/1310942 | Column crop. "the Boxers to destroy the Churches and butcher the Christians of Peking"; "Practically there is no Chinese government now"; the German envoy's ultimatum. |
| `plate_cm0614_hinsang_col.jpg` | *China Mail*, 14 June 1900, p. 2 — https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/image-page/1310949 | Column crop. The chartered transport: "steamer *Hinsang* for Taku", with the paper's question, "Why the whole force did not go by the *Terrible* is a puzzle." |
| `plate_cm0630_refugees_col.jpg` | *China Mail*, 30 June 1900, p. 5 — https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/image-page/1311010 | Column crop. "Missionaries in Peril." and "Seventy-Three Refugees Arrive" — the Japanese steamer *Kudkomaru*. The masthead reads "SATURDAY, JUNE 30, 1900". |
| `plate_cm0717_hsinchi_col.jpg` | *China Mail*, 17 July 1900, p. 2 — https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/image-page/1311067 | Column crop. "REFUGEES FROM TIENTSIN."; the C.M.S. *Hsinchi* freighted with refugees from the ill-fated city; "The First Chinese Contingent"; "Capture of Enemy's Munitions". |
| `plate_cm0727_crisis_col.jpg` | *China Mail*, 27 July 1900, p. 2 — https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/image-page/1311117 | Column crop. "General Massacre Feared in Shansi" — four more English missionaries murdered; McKinley as mediator. |
| `plate_bb1900_volunteer_hl.jpg` | *Blue Book* 1900, p. 59 — https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/image-page/2191712 | Full page. "RETURN OF MILITIA.
