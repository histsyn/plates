## Footnote — the p.16 Chinese box

The tool's seat-collapse guard kept the original full-column box on the Chinese proclamation; it was verified by eye to enclose exactly the 勞／九龍司／巴〔夏禮〕 columns. The G.N. 10 box was painted by hand after a first wrong-region attempt (caught on the probe read) and re-verified.
