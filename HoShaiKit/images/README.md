# HoShaiKit — the image pass

**§6B gate: PASSED (2026-11-21).** Eight production plates. Every box was
painted from the page's own ink, and every box was read by the model at the
**final bytes** of the delivered file, in two combined montages cut from the
painter's `seated_<id>.jpg` (the delivered bytes, not an earlier file).

The gate changed the thread in four ways; all four are recorded in the
notes' §6 corrections register:

1. **The 工商日報 boxes enclose 何世傑 — three characters — not the column
   around it.** The viewer's `ocrBoxes` for a vertical Chinese leaf are whole
   columns; the box was located inside the column by its character pitch.
2. **The Sassoon firm is E. D. Sassoon & Co., not H. D.** The OCR reads
   "N. D."; the scan reads **E** at 14×, beside an **E** control from the same
   typewriter on the same volume ("Executive"). The corpus's own pairing
   witness agrees: *RASHKB Journal* 1992, "Elias David Sassoon, organized
   E.D. Sassoon and Company" (3,175 corpus rows).
3. **The despatch's telegram prints $270,660**, not the OCR's "$270,860".
4. **The syndicate's letter is not a *China Mail* leaf.** It is the
   despatch's own **enclosure**, CO 129/358 p. 381. The *China Mail* leaf
   formerly catalogued for it (viewer 1334133) is **1909-10-07 p6** and does
   not carry the letter; it has been withdrawn from the plate set.

## Two load-bearing documents that have NO image twin

The corpus's image index has **no daily-press and no telegraph series** — the
1930s English press survives in scans only for the *China Mail*. So these two
documents, which carry the thread's central sentences, are **text-only** and
are cited to their public text links (both answer 200):

* ***Daily Press*, 1932-03-12, p8** — the obituary: "Mrs Ho Sai Kit … was the
  wife of **Mr. Ho Sai Kit, the oldest son of the late Mr. Ho Kom Tong**."
  https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/page/2334754
* ***Hongkong Telegraph*, 1932-03-11, p18** — the funeral notice: "The funeral
  of Mr. Ho Sai Kit … from **7 Lower Castle Road** … two priests, Rev. Fr.
  L. D. Ashnessing and the Rev. Fr. Bertin Ashness … A niece is a Sister in
  the French Convent at Taiping."
  https://hkintexts.histsyn.com/page/2946920

The *China Mail* image card for 1932-03-12 carries three leaves; their OCR has
no "Ho", no "Sai Kit", no "Kom Tong" and no "OBITUARY" — the *China Mail* did
not print the same items. **Read and recorded: the scans do not support these
two documents, and the corpus's own index says why.**

## The gate's own contract

The painter emits `<stem>.boxes.json` per plate — for every box the phrase it
encloses, the unit (characters on vertical type, lines on horizontal type) and
the count. `scripts/check_plate_manifest.py HoShaiKit` compares the manifest's
verdicts against that data: the caption must quote the boxed phrase, the
"boxes" column must state the box count and the unit counts, and a verdict
whose unit of account is COLUMNS is a FAIL. The painter's own numbers are the
authority; the manifest cannot describe a plate it has not measured.

The plate files are hosted at `histsyn.github.io/plates/HoShaiKit/` and
md5-verified against the local bytes before the paper is built.
