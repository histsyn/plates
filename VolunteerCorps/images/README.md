## Facebook-only photograph (not cited in the paper)

`fb_hkvdc_3rd_company_1941.jpg` — the 3rd Company of the Hong Kong Volunteer Defence Corps in 1941, photographer uncredited, supplied by the Hong Kong Military Service Corps (dissolved), public domain. Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1941_Hong_Kong_Volunteer_Defence_Corps_3rd_Company_before_death.jpg (accessed 23 November 2026). Read by the session model at the final JPEG bytes: the full group is visible, with no lettering introduced. It is a photograph, not a document plate, and carries no highlight box. It is used only as the single Facebook attachment, not as a `figure.plate` in the paper.
