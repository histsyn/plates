## Notes

* **Raw scans** are kept in `raw/` (file names `raw_<image-id>.jpg`); `plate_TEMP_1868_ad_zoom.jpg` is a working zoom of the 1868 section (not a production file).
* **The inscriptions are NOT plated**: the `inscriptions-image` series is login-gated (no `imagePath` served — the same gate the LooAking thread recorded) and the 香港碑銘彙編 is a modern compilation; the stone texts cited in the thread (1727 burner 「大清國廣東廣州新安縣泰坑本鄉」; 1904/1911 同胞 couplets; 1910 華商燕梳) are quoted from the text series and the images are used for verification only, never republished.
* Hosted copies: `https://histsyn.github.io/plates/ChineseNaming_Qing/images/<file>` (pushed 2026-10-29, md5-verified against these files).
* All sources are Hong Kong Government Gazettes / the *China Mail* / the *Directory and Chronicle* / 工商日報-published government notices of the 1850s–1890s — public-domain by age; the compilations involved (none plated) are avoided.
